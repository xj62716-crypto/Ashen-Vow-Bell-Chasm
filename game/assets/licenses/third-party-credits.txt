v0.10 production update (2026-09-14)
The credited CC0 arm mesh is retargeted to a 48-bone deform rig with 24 authored
Blender actions. The Godot import adds one root bone. All new character armor,
weapons, architecture modules, shaders, and tempered_armor / charcoal_weave /
riven_wood surface maps are produced within this project. Existing Poly Haven
surface maps remain subject to their CC0 credits above. Combat sounds remix
the already credited Kenney CC0 source packs; source recipes are documented
in docs/combat-audio-manifest.json. Music is unchanged.
Ghostrunner reference frames remain in documentation only. No Ghostrunner
models, animations, textures, audio, or archive contents are distributed.
